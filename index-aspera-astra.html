What I Offer

Services

I am a clairsentient, clairvoyant, and clairaudient spiritual channel, acting as a bridge between the spirit world and the physical world. During a reading, I connect with spirit and intuitively explore your energetic landscape to identify areas where energy may be blocked or out of alignment. I also offer mediumship services, connecting with your loved ones who have passed to bring through any messages they may have for you.

These blockages can stem from many sources, including past experiences, limiting beliefs, unresolved emotions, family patterns, or what some traditions describe as karmic lessons carried from this life or others. Often, the stories we tell ourselves become the very barriers that prevent us from moving forward.

What comes through in a reading is not necessarily a prediction of the future. More often, it is guidance about what you need to understand, heal, or embrace in the present moment. The messages are intended to help you gain clarity, release what no longer serves you, and align more fully with your highest path and purpose.

I believe the future is fluid rather than fixed. We are here to learn, grow, and evolve, and life's lessons can appear in many different forms. A reading offers insight into the energies and opportunities surrounding you, empowering you to make conscious choices that shape your own journey.

The Space Between

About

Hello, my name is Aspera Astra and I am an intuitive energy reader. My name means "through hardship to the stars," and it reflects my belief that our deepest wounds often hold the keys to our greatest transformation. I work as a conduit, not a director: holding space for what wants to emerge in you.

Two years ago, during an Ayahuasca ceremony, I experienced a profound spiritual awakening that changed the course of my life. During that experience, I made contact with spirit guides who called me to a path of service. What began as a personal transformation evolved into a deep connection with the spirit world and a commitment to helping others.

Since then, I have dedicated myself to developing this connection through meditation, spiritual practice, and direct work with my guides. Today, I offer services and spiritual guidance to help people navigate life's challenges, transitions, relationships, and questions of purpose.

My practice weaves intuitive perception with energetic healing, honoring both the seen and unseen dimensions of your experience. Every session is unique, guided by what your spirit is ready to receive. I approach every session with compassion, honesty, and respect. My role is not to tell you what to believe or what decisions to make, but to share the messages, insights, and perspectives that come through so that you can move forward with greater clarity and confidence.

Thank you for your trust and support. I am honored to be part of your journey.
